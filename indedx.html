How we work

Most of what we supply goes into LT panels, machines and plant upgrades around Navi Mumbai. The questions we get are specific: a rating, a make, a delivery date. Our job is to answer those quickly and correctly.

We keep a defined set of principals rather than chasing every brand, so that when a drawing specifies a make, we can supply it as specified instead of pushing an alternative.

What we stand on

Genuine product with documentation. Stock held locally so a routine item does not become a three-week wait. Named people you can reach directly. And enough product knowledge to catch a mis-specified item before it ships.
